# Demo video

[Watch the 75-second IsaacMin walkthrough](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/511295b4-9c28-440c-996d-b0efe4071160).

The video shows actual preset selection, OpenClaw/NVIDIA agent construction,
Isaac ground and aerial views, Minecraft/Isaac comparisons, and a completed
world download. Build and rendering waits are condensed. The construction
animation is the app's labelled simplified preview of real build data.

The featured Jungle Rivers conversion completed all eleven agent-dispatched
stages and eight native Isaac captures. Its full 13.0 GB browser download
matched the produced archive's hash. The depicted region is 256 m across;
this walkthrough does not establish robot navigation performance or successful
rendering at larger scales.

Voice: **NVIDIA Magpie TTS Multilingual, Ray**
(`Magpie-Multilingual.EN-US.Ray`). [Official model](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/magpie-tts-multilingual).
Original narration; speech playback adjusted to 96% speed.
